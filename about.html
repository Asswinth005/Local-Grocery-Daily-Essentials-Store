What Drives Us

Our core principles

Three simple promises we make to every customer, every delivery, every day.

01 — Fairness

Fair to farmers, fair to you

We pay our partner farms above-market rates and pass the savings to you by cutting out middlemen and waste.

02 — Sustainability

Zero-waste by design

From compostable packaging to electric delivery vans, every choice is made with the planet in mind.

03 — Convenience

Freshness without friction

Same-day slots, live tracking, and a checkout that takes seconds — because your time matters.

Milestones

From a single stall to city-wide.

Every year we've grown a little more — always with the same obsession for freshness and fairness.

2019 Riverside Farmers Market

The first stall

Two friends, one rented table, and a crate of just-picked tomatoes. We sold out in 40 minutes — and knew we were onto something.

2021 First delivery van

Going mobile

We launched our first same-day delivery route, bringing farm boxes to 200 households across three neighbourhoods.

2023 Carbon-neutral hubs

Dark stores, bright future

Opened our first micro-fulfillment hubs, switched to 100% renewable energy, and eliminated single-use plastic from all packaging.

2026 35+ farm partners

A growing family

Today, GreenCart serves 12,000+ households and works directly with 35 local farms — and we're just getting started.
