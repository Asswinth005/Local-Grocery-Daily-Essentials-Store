Questions

Delivery FAQs

Everything you need to know about how we deliver.

We currently deliver to three zones: Express Core (Downtown, Midtown, Riverside), Standard Metro (Northside, Westend, Eastgate), and Suburban Loop (Willow Creek, Meadowbrook). Enter your postcode above to check if you're covered — we're adding new zones every month.

Delivery fees vary by zone: Express Core is $2.90 (free over $25), Standard Metro is $4.50 (free over $40), and Suburban Loop is $6.90 (free over $60). Silver and Gold loyalty members get automatic fee reductions on every order.

Yes! Every order comes with live GPS tracking. You'll see your driver's location on a map, get an accurate ETA, and receive notifications when they're nearby. You can also contact your driver directly through the app.

No problem. You can choose contact-free delivery at checkout. Your driver will leave the order in a safe spot and send you a photo confirmation. For chilled items, we recommend leaving a cooler bag outside if you won't be home within 30 minutes.

We deliver 7 days a week, including most public holidays. Weekend delivery windows are the same as weekdays, though Express Core slots may fill up faster on Saturday and Sunday mornings. We recommend booking your slot early.

We stand behind every item. If anything is missing or not up to our freshness standard, contact us within 24 hours and we'll refund it instantly — no return trip needed. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.
